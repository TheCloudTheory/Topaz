# Licensing and pricing

Topaz's licensing model is changing with version 1.11. The [pricing page](https://topaz.thecloudtheory.com/pricing/) describes four tiers. All tiers include the same Topaz features; eligibility, quotas, support, and licensing requirements differ.

## Choose a tier

| Tier | Price | Who it is for | Limits and support |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Community | Free | Non-commercial projects | No SLA; support through GitHub issues. |
| Free | $0 | Commercial use by companies with consolidated annual revenue of $1 million or less | One Azure subscription and 10 resource groups; no service-level agreement (SLA); support through GitHub issues. |
| Commercial | $49 per named seat per year | Companies above $1 million in consolidated annual revenue | No quotas or usage-based fees; dedicated support. |
| Enterprise | Custom annual agreement | Teams needing enterprise terms | Includes Commercial features, a dedicated shared-mode license, private builds, dedicated support, an SLA, and input on the product roadmap. |

## Shared-mode deployments

Shared-mode deployments require a separate dedicated license. Enterprise agreements include this license. Contact Topaz to discuss a dedicated shared-mode license or Enterprise terms.

## Pricing details

- Community and Free include all features, including control planes, data planes, Chaos Mode, and background services.
- Commercial licenses are billed per named seat each year; there are no usage-based fees.
- The Free tier's revenue threshold is based on consolidated annual revenue.
- Enterprise pricing is set through an annual agreement.

## Which terms apply?

This page summarizes the published pricing information; it is not a license grant. Check the license and terms provided with the Topaz version you use. For questions about commercial or shared-mode licensing, use the contact options on the [pricing page](https://topaz.thecloudtheory.com/pricing/).
